      Advanced action — read before proceeding
      
      
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
        
      ⚠ This is outside the normal weekly workflow
      
        
      
          Normally, your baseline is saved automatically when you click
          📊 Baseline & Generate GM Report (Step 5).
          That ensures your diff always reflects exactly what your GM saw in the last report.
        
      
      
      
      
      
        Use this only if you need to reset the baseline mid-week — for example,
        after a major pipeline restructure that you want to treat as the new reference point.
      
      
      
      
        
      ⚠ Impact on What Changed
      
        
      
          ⇄ What Changed will compare live data against right now,
          not against your last GM Report. Your next PPT diff may not reflect the full
          week's pipeline movement.
